Depozit.uz, “Korzinka.uz asoschisi – Zafar Xoshimovdan biznesga oid iqtiboslar” (compilation of his business rules)

  • The most important thing in business is not knowing good answers, but asking the right questions.

    Biznesda eng muhimi — yaxshi javoblarni bilish emas, balki to‘g‘ri savollar berishdir.

    Zafar Khashimov
  • I always do my shopping at korzinka.uz. You must taste your own business.

    Men doim xaridlarni korzinka.uz dan amalga oshiraman. Siz, albatta, o‘z biznesingizni mazasini tatib ko‘rishingiz kerak.

    Zafar Khashimov
Every successful entrepreneur was buying a bank, so we decided to as well. Truth is, we had no idea what the banking business even was. Vyacheslav Kim
