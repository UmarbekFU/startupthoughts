Alberuni’s India, c. 1030, Preface, trans. Edward C. Sachau

  • No one will deny that in questions of historic authenticity hearsay does not equal eye-witness. Al-Biruni
  • Everything which exists on this subject in our literature is second-hand information which one has copied from the other, a farrago of materials never sifted by the sieve of critical examination. Al-Biruni
  • This book is not a polemical one. My book is nothing but a simple historic record of facts. Al-Biruni
  • That man only is praiseworthy who shrinks from a lie and always adheres to the truth, enjoying credit even among liars. Al-Biruni
