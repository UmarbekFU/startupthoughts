The Book of Words, Word Thirty-Three

  • If you want to be rich, learn a trade. Wealth diminishes with time, but a skill does not.

    Мал жұтайды, өнер жұтамайды.

    Abai Kunanbaev
  • He who sells the fruits of his labor without trying to deceive is considered saintly by the people.

    Алдау қоспай адал еңбегін сатқан қолөнерлі — қазақтың әулиесі сол.

    Abai Kunanbaev
While you are seeking happiness, everybody wishes you well; but once you have attained it, your only well-wisher is yourself. Abai Kunanbaev
