Dīwān Lughāt al-Turk, 1072–74 — Turkic proverb recorded by al-Kashgari, with his own Arabic gloss

  • If there is quarrelling at the plowing, there will be no fighting at the threshing. Settle the matter at the start of the dealing, so no dispute appears at the end.

    Sabanda sandrış bolsa, örtgünde irtiş bolmas

    Mahmud al-Kashgari
  • By the tongue you reach the table. It is said of the man who is told to make his worth known by speaking.

    Tilin tergige tegir

    Mahmud al-Kashgari
