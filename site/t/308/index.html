It seems that perfection is attained not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing more to take away.

Il semble que la perfection soit atteinte non quand il n’y a plus rien à ajouter, mais quand il n’y a plus rien à retrancher.

Antoine de Saint-Exupéry
from Terre des hommes (Wind, Sand and Stars), 1939 ↗
I asked counsel from all, but I considered the good and the evil of every opinion, and from each I selected those things which were just and expedient. Amir Timur
