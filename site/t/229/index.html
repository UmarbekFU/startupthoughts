Such are the merchants. So consort with them, leave the door open. Treat them kindly and you may be sure that your good name will travel far. For these are they who spread one's name abroad, be it good or bad.

Yusuf Balasaguni
from Kutadgu Bilig (Wisdom of Royal Glory), chapter 58, Associating with merchants, p. 184, trans. Robert Dankoff, University of Chicago Press 1983 ↗
