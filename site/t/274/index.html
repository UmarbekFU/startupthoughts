When he buys, he calls the linen coarse cotton; when he sells, he finds a hundred words to set coarse cotton above linen.

Olurda katonni bo‘z deb, sotarda bo‘z vasfida katondin ortug‘ yuz so‘z deb.

Alisher Navoi
from Mahbub ul-Qulub (Beloved of Hearts), 1500, twenty-seventh chapter, On those who buy and sell in the city. Uzbek text verified in the edition linked; the English is a compressed rendering of it, as no standard published English translation of this passage was found. ↗
