  • With each proof I examined, I set down the premisses and put them in order in a file, then examined what deductions might be drawn from them. Ibn Sina (Avicenna)
  • Those of the chemical craft know well that no change can be effected in the different species of substances, though they can produce the appearance of such change. Ibn Sina (Avicenna)
  • The knowledge of anything is not acquired or complete unless it is known by its causes. Ibn Sina (Avicenna)
