Yusuf Balasaguni

  • Haste is the head of every ill-begotten act, deliberation the companion of every deed well done. Yusuf Balasaguni
  • Whatever you undertake, consider what the issue will be, for if you fail to reckon the expense, it will turn out to be a loss. Yusuf Balasaguni
  • Consultation is the medicine prescribed for every action proposed. Consult with a near one and your undertaking will prosper, fail to consult and you will rue the day. Yusuf Balasaguni
  • Whatever you wish to undertake, first consult your own heart, only then other people; and if your heart is not pleased with it, then stay away from it. Yusuf Balasaguni
  • As long as the wise man does not bring out wisdom upon his tongue, his wisdom may lie hidden for years and shed no light. Yusuf Balasaguni
  • Whenever much labor is invested in a thing it becomes loved and desired. Yusuf Balasaguni
  • Seek not life alone, but also a good name; for you will die soon, but your name will remain behind. Yusuf Balasaguni
  • One who begins his enterprise with knowledge will gain his desire in the end, no matter what it may be. Yusuf Balasaguni
  • Treat them kindly and you may be sure that your good name will travel far. For these are they who spread one’s name abroad, be it good or bad.

    Such are the merchants. So consort with them, leave the door open. Treat them kindly and you may be sure that your good name will travel far. For these are they who spread one's name abroad, be it good or bad.

    Yusuf Balasaguni
  • If they do some work for you, pay their wages promptly, and give them liberally of food and drink, lest you earn a bad name among the people.

    Associate with them, make them your friends, and you will live a happy life. If they do some work for you, pay their wages promptly, and give them liberally of food and drink, lest you earn a bad name among the people.

    Yusuf Balasaguni
  • He ought to be close-fisted rather than open-handed. Then the treasury won’t be squandered. Liberality is a fine thing in its place, but not with another man’s property! Yusuf Balasaguni
  • When he borrows he must pay back promptly, so that others will readily lend to him when he asks them. As long as he is flexible in transactions and his conduct is upright, he will not lack for funds when he needs them.

    In the matter of loans he should be flexible, greeting great and small with sweet words. But when he borrows he must pay back promptly, so that others will readily lend to him when he asks them. As long as he is flexible in transactions and his conduct is upright, he will not lack for funds when he needs them.

    Yusuf Balasaguni
